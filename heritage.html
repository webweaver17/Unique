GERARD® HERITAGE ROOF TILES

With its scalloped profile and choice of natural colours, it is easy to see why Gerard® Heritage roof tiles are the world’s best-selling stone-coated steel roof tile.

CHOOSE BOND

DESIGN

More than 50 years of research and development has gone into Bond Tiles. The Gerard® Bond roofing panel is pressure formed from steel, seven tile impressions are formed horizontally in one panel. Each panel is profiled to form an extremely strong overlapping and interlocking structure which is aesthetically pleasing.

NATURAL STONE

Gerard® Roofing tiles are coated with natural stone chips, volcanically formed and taken from quarries in New Zealand. These coatings add natural beauty to the roof and a hard layer of protection from the weather. Being of natural stone, the vibrant colour is protected against the extreme UV of the African sun and will never change.

ALU-ZINC STEEL

All Gerard® roof products are manufactured from alu-zinc steel which have shown in tests to last 6-9 times longer than ordinary galvanised steel roofing products. This is achieved by protecting the steel core with zinc, which is itself protected by an aluminium barrier. As a pioneer of using alu-zinc steel, Gerard® has unrivalled experience in long lasting steel roof tile manufacture.
